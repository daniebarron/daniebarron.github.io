About

I am an MSc student in Mathematical and Theoretical Physics at the University of Oxford, having previously studied Theoretical Physics at Trinity College Dublin. I am interested in condensed matter theory, quantum many-body physics, and the connections between statistical mechanics and quantum field theory.

My current dissertation, supervised by Dr Luca Rüegg, concerns the stability of generalised Laughlin wavefunctions in spatially nonuniform magnetic fields. I am investigating this using diffusion Monte Carlo, with particular attention to variational and fixed-phase energies.

Research experience

2026–present · University of Oxford

Stability of generalised Laughlin wavefunctions

Master's dissertation with Dr Luca Rüegg. I am using diffusion Monte Carlo to study generalised Laughlin states in nonuniform magnetic fields, including how the energy comparison changes with field inhomogeneity and Landau-level mixing. This work is ongoing.

2026 · Tyndall National Institute

Atomistic modelling and machine learning

Summer fellowship supervised by Dr Michael Nolan. I worked on machine-learning approaches to PFAS spectroscopy and on extending simulations of transition-metal nitride oxidation using machine-learned interatomic potentials.

2026 · Trinity College Dublin / Hamilton Trust

Keldysh field theory for lattice population dynamics

With O. Daniel O'Carroll, supervised by Prof Karen Wiltshire. We studied the Keldysh formalism and its classical stochastic limit, including Langevin and Fokker–Planck equations and possible applications to population dynamics.

2025–2026 · Trinity College Dublin

Higher-dimensional integrability and twistor actions

Undergraduate dissertation supervised by Prof Tristan McLoughlin. I examined dimensional reductions of holomorphic Chern–Simons theory and investigated compatibility of the Atiyah–Ward ansatz with the required translation symmetry.

Dissertation · Poster · Slides

2025 · Dublin Institute for Advanced Studies

Dimer models as models of Majorana fermions

Summer studentship supervised by Prof Denjoe O'Connor. I investigated dimer models on several two-dimensional lattices using Kasteleyn methods, with attention to critical behaviour and connections with Majorana fermions and Ising conformal field theory.

2025 · Trinity College Dublin / Hamilton Trust

Topology in the quantum Hall effect and non-Abelian anyons

Project supervised by Dr Chaolun Wu, examining fractional quantum Hall states, the Laughlin and Moore–Read wavefunctions, anyonic excitations, and their topological descriptions.
